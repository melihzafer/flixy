My recommendation
✓ Pick
Coral Fire + Playfair Italic / Space Grotesk
The warmth of coral reads as "play" energy and maps perfectly to cinema seat red. Playfair italic titles give every film a mini-poster moment — editorial, A24-esque, confident. Space Grotesk provides clean utility without looking like every other app.
If you want more edge
Swap Coral for Electric Chartreuse + keep the type pairing. Louder, more subversive — closer to a zine aesthetic. Works especially well if Flixy wants to skew younger and lean into the "unconventional pick" angle.
If you want more drama
Cinema Magenta + Bebas Neue. Maximalist film-festival energy. Loud and theatrical. Risk: can feel heavy on OLED black; needs very careful use of the accent.
