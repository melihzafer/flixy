# Release notes

## 0.1.0

The first cut of Flixy.

- Swipe-based discovery deck powered by your taste.
- Watchlist with priority and "already seen" states.
- Filter by genre, provider, runtime, year. Mood presets for quick picks.
- Streaming availability per country, one tap to open the right app.
- Push notifications for weekly picks and new-for-you alerts (opt-in).
- Available in English, Türkçe, and Български.
