Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-28

This Privacy Policy explains what information Flixy collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at hello@flixy.app.

1. What we collect

  • Account information. Email address (when you create an account) and an opaque user identifier. Anonymous sessions store only a device-scoped identifier.
  • Taste signals. The titles you swipe on, your watchlist, and your filter preferences. We use these to personalize recommendations.
  • Device information. Operating system, app version, country, and language for diagnostics and crash reports.
  • Push tokens. If you enable notifications, we store an APNs or FCM token to deliver them.

2. How we use it

  • To deliver and personalize the Flixy experience.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve performance.
  • To send transactional emails (account confirmation, password reset) and notifications you opt into.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with processors who help operate Flixy: hosting (Supabase, Vercel), error reporting (Sentry), product analytics (PostHog), email delivery (Resend), and movie/show metadata (TMDB, Watchmode). Each is bound by a data processing agreement.

4. Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing privacy@flixy.app. We will respond within 30 days.
  • You can disable push notifications from your device settings at any time.

5. Children

Flixy is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be announced in-app.
